title: "Elegant Solutions"
date: 2011-12-06T05:58:00Z
slug: elegant-solutions

---

I like elegant solutions because they are easier to administer. In fact, I
believe that a solution is truly elegant if you do not even need to actively
administer it. One thing to note about elegance though is that it's hard to
achieve if you're harried, if you're out of time, trying to deal with too many
things at time, etc. So it's important to be aware of you current state of
mind and be sure that you are focused. Otherwise, you'll never achieve that
elegant solution you have in mind.
